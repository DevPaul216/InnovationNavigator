Decision Rationale: Explain why this choice is the strongest based on:

- Which factors were most decisive in your judgment
- Any trade-offs or weaknesses you acknowledge

Next Steps: Suggest what should happen next if this recommendation is pursued (e.g., further testing, pilot, immediate implementation, etc.)
